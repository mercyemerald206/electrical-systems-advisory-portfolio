## Reliability vs Cost Model

| Option | Cost | Reliability | Recommendation |
|------|------|------------|---------------|
| Low redundancy | Low | Low | Not recommended |
| Medium redundancy | Medium | Medium | Balanced |
| High redundancy | High | High | Critical systems only |
