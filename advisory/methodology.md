# Advisory Methodology

## 1. Problem Framing
Define the engineering challenge with clarity:
- What is failing?
- What constraints exist?
- What data is available?

---

## 2. System Decomposition
Break system into components:
- Electrical subsystems
- Data flows
- Failure points

---

## 3. Trade-off Analysis
Evaluate:
- Cost vs reliability
- Performance vs scalability
- Accuracy vs interpretability

---

## 4. Risk Assessment
Identify:
- Failure modes
- Uncertainty levels
- Operational impact

---

## 5. Recommendation
Deliver:
- Clear action steps
- Justified decisions
- Scalable solutions
