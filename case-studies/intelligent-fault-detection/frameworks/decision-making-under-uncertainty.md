# Decision Making Under Uncertainty

## Step 1: Define Known vs Unknown
- What data is available?
- What is missing?

## Step 2: Build Hypotheses
- Multiple possible failure causes

## Step 3: Prioritize by Impact
- Which failure has highest risk?

## Step 4: Test Efficiently
- Minimum effort validation

## Step 5: Decide with Trade-offs
- No perfect answer — optimize outcome

---

## Principle

> The best engineers do not wait for perfect data — they make optimal decisions with imperfect data.
